1. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
JMAP Connect is a free email app for people who use Fastmail, a paid email service. Fastmail has its own app, but many of its customers want a fast, native app that feels at home on iPhone, iPad and Mac. JMAP Connect talks to Fastmail over JMAP, the open email standard Fastmail runs on (RFC 8620 and RFC 8621), so mail loads quickly and only what changed is fetched. It has no ads, no tracking and no in app purchases.

2. HOW TO USE IT (NO ACCOUNT NEEDED)
- Open the app and tap Try it with sample mail on the welcome screen. This loads made up emails that live only inside the app; nothing is sent anywhere.
- Every main feature works with sample mail: the inbox and folders, opening a conversation, reply, reply all, forward, new message, attachments, star, archive, move, delete, mark as spam, search (it looks in every folder), and Settings (signatures, default From address, notifications).
- To leave sample mail, open Settings and tap Remove next to Sample mail.
- Real accounts: tap Add your Fastmail account and paste a Fastmail API token. The app never asks for a password. Fastmail customers make a token in Fastmail under Settings, Privacy & Security, Manage API tokens. We can't provide a demo Fastmail login because Fastmail sign ups need a verified phone number, which is why sample mail exists.
- Accounts: the app does not create accounts. People add an existing Fastmail account and can remove it at any time in Settings, which deletes its token from the Keychain and its data from the device.

3. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Fastmail's JMAP API (api.fastmail.com), the only service the app needs. The device talks straight to Fastmail; there is no JMAP Connect server.
- Gravatar (gravatar.com) for senders' profile pictures. This can be turned off in Settings.
- Pictures inside emails load from the senders' own web servers, as in any email app. This can be turned off in Settings.
No analytics, advertising, AI, payment or other sign in services are used.

4. REGIONS
The app works the same in every region where it's offered. There are no regional differences in features or content.

5. REGULATED INDUSTRY OR THIRD PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. JMAP Connect is an independent app. It uses Fastmail's public JMAP API, which Fastmail documents for third party apps at fastmail.com/dev. It isn't made by or affiliated with Fastmail, and the listing says so. It contains no third party content.
